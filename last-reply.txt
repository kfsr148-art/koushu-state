Frame 165 is built and pushed as panel v226. I'm waiting on its cloud check, so the card isn't out yet.

- **What it shows:** under the "返事パネル" title, while a job is running, there are two lines. The first is a red bold estimate, "見込み 約N%", or "見込み —" when there is no data yet. The second is a small gray "今まで M%", or "1%未満" when under one percent. Nothing shows when there is no job. No dollar amount.
- **Where the numbers come from:**
  - **Estimate:** it is the median weekly-percentage cost of finished jobs with a similar time estimate, and only appears once at least three such jobs have been recorded.
  - **So far:** it is the weekly percentage now minus the weekly percentage when the job started.
  - **History:** finished jobs are saved to `~/.claude/job-week-history.tsv`, which also starts the usage-learning that the shelf needs.
